# MRG Infrastructure Suite — Master Service Agreement
### White-Label Reseller Terms & Custom Domain Mapping Regulations

This document governs the infrastructure allocation constraints for White-Label Resellers, Managed Service Providers (MSPs), and B2B enterprise partners mapping custom domains onto the MRG cloud proxy mesh.

---

## ⚖️ 1. Wholesale Compute Billing Allocations
* **Base Wholesale Rate Model:** All white-label reseller nodes are charged a rigid infrastructure base rate of $0.1200 USD per computed processing minute run under their custom mapped domains.
* **Metered Settlements:** Fees are tracked by the minute via our unified transactional database ledgers and debited automatically from the reseller's pre-funded account balance. 
* **Suspension Thresholds:** If the pre-funded account balance sinks beneath $0.00 USD, custom domain proxy routing paths are automatically paused within 60 seconds to protect the 10-region host cluster from resource depletion.

---

## 🔒 2. Data Protection, Privacy & COPPA Compliance
* **Edge-AI Data Isolation:** Resellers acknowledge that the core engine operates an isolated edge framework. Private operational telemetry and patient workflow data frames are kept fully separate from generic public cloud indexes.
* **Junior Academy Safeguards:** Any reseller offering services targeted at students or minors must enforce absolute privacy defaults, strictly blocking third-party telemetry scripts to ensure comprehensive compliance with COPPA and GDPR regulations.

---

## 🌐 3. Custom Domain & DNS Mapping Policies
* **SSL/TLS Certificates:** When an MSP maps a custom domain pointer, the MRG global edge proxy automatically provisions an encrypted SSL/TLS certificate via an on-demand Let's Encrypt pipeline.
* **Indemnification Boundaries:** Resellers assume full liability for the content, configuration, and marketing actions broadcasted under their custom branded domains. MRG acts strictly as a headless backend computelayer.
