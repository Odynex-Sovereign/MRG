Accessibility commitment

We design core flows to meet WCAG 2.2 Level AA where feasible: semantic structure, keyboard access, visible focus, sufficient contrast, and labels.

What is in place

  • Skip link to main content
  • Landmark regions and heading hierarchy
  • Visible :focus-visible styles
  • Form labels and descriptive errors
  • Non-color-only status where practical
  • prefers-reduced-motion respected for decorative animation

Known limitations

Dense data views and future charting will need continued testing with screen readers and high-contrast modes. Auth providers (when configured) inherit their own accessibility quality.

Feedback

If you encounter a barrier, tell us what page, what assistive technology, and what you expected. We prioritize fixes that unblock core tasks.
