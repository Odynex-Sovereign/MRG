# Support and service-level options (template)

These are planning options, not commitments until written into an agreement.

| Level | Channels | Hours | Initial response target | Typical fit |
|-------|----------|-------|-------------------------|-------------|
| Community | Docs, feedback form | Best effort | N/A | Free / Personal |
| Standard | Email | Business hours | 1–2 business days | Pro / Team |
| Priority | Email + scheduled calls | Extended business | Same business day | Team / Nonprofit |
| Enterprise | Named contact, ticket system | As contracted | As contracted (e.g. 4h Sev1) | Enterprise / Government |

## Severity examples

- **Sev1:** Service unreachable for all users of a dedicated instance  
- **Sev2:** Major feature broken with workaround  
- **Sev3:** Minor defect or question  

## Maintenance windows

Scheduled maintenance communicated in advance for dedicated deployments; SaaS follows platform norms.
