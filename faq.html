Frequently asked questions

What is MRG?

MRG is an integrated simulation suite for practicing composure and decision-making under rare, high-impact pressure. It is designed for individuals, teams, and institutions.

Is this predictive software?

No. MRG does not claim to predict black swan events. It helps you practice responses so you are better prepared if something unusual happens.

Do I need an account?

You can try the public demo without an account. Free and paid plans use a simple onboarding flow. Full SSO is available for enterprise deployments that configure it.

Are the metrics real?

Demo and sandbox metrics are labeled simulated. Deployments that connect approved models and sensors can produce richer feedback; those integrations are optional and documented.

How does billing work?

Public plans are listed on the Pricing page. Checkout remains in demo mode until an operator configures Stripe secrets on the server. We never charge without that configuration.

Can my organization buy a perpetual license?

Yes — see Institutional licensing. A buy-out is a negotiated perpetual license with defined rights and support years. It does not automatically transfer IP ownership unless the contract says so.

Is the product accessible?

We aim for WCAG 2.2 AA on core flows: semantic HTML, keyboard navigation, visible focus, contrast, and labels. See the Accessibility page for status and how to report issues.
