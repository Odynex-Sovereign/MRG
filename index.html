How a session works

Clear steps. No jargon required.

1. Choose a scenario

Supply shock, cyber cascade, macro policy event, or a custom drill.

2. Decide under pressure

Work through timed steps. The system records choices and composure signals on eligible plans.

3. Debrief and improve

Review what went well, where stress rose, and what you would do differently next time.
