FAQ

Straightforward answers about billing, data, and deployment.

Is payment processed on this site?

No. When Stripe is configured, you are redirected to Stripe-hosted Checkout. Card details are handled by Stripe, not by MRG application servers.

What happens if Stripe is not configured?

The API returns a clear sandbox response with no checkout URL. The UI shows an informational message and does not claim that a license was purchased.

Can I cancel or change plans?

Yes. Subscriptions managed through Stripe can be canceled or modified according to the terms shown at checkout and in your Stripe customer portal (when enabled).

Do you store payment card data?

No. Payment instruments are handled by the payment provider. MRG may store non-sensitive references such as customer or subscription IDs after a verified webhook.

Is this a regulated financial product?

No. MRG provides simulation and decision-training software. It is not a bank, broker, insurer, or investment advisor, and it does not process customer funds beyond software license fees.

How do enterprise / custom contracts work?

Contact the team with volume, region, SSO, and SLA requirements. Custom retainers are quoted offline and are not completed through the self-serve checkout buttons.
