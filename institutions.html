Institutional licensing & buy-out

For municipalities, public agencies, schools, universities, nonprofits, healthcare organizations, and corporations. All figures below are illustrative and subject to a written agreement and legal review. A buy-out grants a perpetual license under contract terms — it does not automatically transfer ownership of intellectual property unless the negotiated agreement expressly says so.

1. Annual SaaS license

Per-user or organization-tier pricing, hosted by us (or your chosen region). Includes defined support level, hosting model, and renewal terms.

Best for: ongoing training programs that prefer operational simplicity.

2. Dedicated / private deployment

Customer-specific Fly.io, cloud, or customer-hosted instance. Optional data residency, migration, training, and maintenance packages.

Best for: regulated environments and data-control requirements.

3. Perpetual buy-out license

One-time source and deployment license with a defined scope of rights, optional escrow, and a set number of support / update / security-maintenance years. Optional annual maintenance after the included period.

Explicit exclusions: third-party costs, cloud spend, custom feature development, legal/compliance certification, and external service fees — unless scoped separately.
