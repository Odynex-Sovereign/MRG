Institutional licensing & buy-out

Illustrative ranges only. A buy-out is a perpetual license under contract — not automatic IP ownership transfer unless the agreement says so.

1. Annual SaaS

Per-user or org-tier, hosted by us, defined support and renewal.

2. Dedicated deployment

Private Fly/cloud/customer-hosted. Optional residency and training.

3. Perpetual buy-out

One-time license, defined rights, optional escrow and support years. Cloud costs excluded unless scoped.
